# Reminder's purpose

Reminder brings daily reminders, the Quran, hadith and the Names of Allah together.
Those four experiences take priority. The texts must remain easy to find, read,
listen to and return to. More resources should deepen that experience rather
than crowd it out.

Search helps readers find passages. Ask AI adds source-backed summaries.
The shared model supports deeper understanding by consulting Arabic language
resources, translations and attributed tafsir. A translation alone is not a
complete account of the Arabic, and a root alone does not determine meaning.
Explanations should make the evidence and interpretive choices understandable.

Reminder is not another broad knowledge or general AI-chat destination.
Aslam.org serves that broader purpose. Avoid adding an AI home, promotional
feed, separate chat destination or prominent linguistic dashboard to Reminder.

## Contextual explanations

A reader should not need to copy a passage into Google or Aslam to understand it.
The intended entry points are a selected passage, a verse or a chapter. Explain
opens in the reading context and clearly identifies its subject. Arabic word
forms, roots, grammar, translations and tafsir are supporting detail, revealed
when useful rather than placed ahead of the text.

A follow-up such as “Why is this translated that way?” remains attached to the
same passage. This is a focused explanation, not a new general conversation.
Chapter-level explanations must distinguish a supported overview from an
exhaustive analysis; never imply that a few retrieved verses cover every theme.

These are product constraints, not a claim that the contextual panel has shipped.
Current work prepares the evidence and API underneath that future interaction.

## Evidence rules

Keep source identities and disagreements visible. Do not invent missing roots,
join incompatible word numbering, or silently replace one interpretation with
another. Model explanations cite their sources and distinguish quotation,
translation, lexical meaning, tafsir and generated interpretation. Reading and
ordinary search must remain useful without a model provider.
