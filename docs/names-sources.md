# Names text corrections

The existing 99 names, Arabic strings, numbering and order are retained.

Descriptions for entries 10, 17, 37, 39, 48, 65, 68, 80 and 82 are concise
paraphrases of the respective entries in al-Ghazali, *Al-Maqsad al-Asna*,
as translated by Robert Charles Stade, *Ninety-Nine Names of God in Islam*
(Daystar Press, 1970):
https://www.ghazali.org/books/Ghazali-99-Name-of-God.pdf

Relevant PDF pages (one-based): 15 (Mutakabbir), 38 (Kabeer), 40 (Muqeet
and its comparison with Razzaaq), 46 and 53 (Majeed/Maajid), 53 (Samad),
57 (Tawwaab), 58 (Afuww and its comparison with Ghafoor).
These are editorial paraphrases, not quotations or a new authentication of
the complete enumeration. Existing longer summaries are not newly verified.

The transliterations Wadood, Muhyee and Mu’akhkhir follow the Arabic forms
الودود، المحيي، المؤخر using the app's existing simplified vowel convention.
The forms can also be checked in Jami at-Tirmidhi 3507:
https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3507
That enumeration is graded weak; it is not cited as proof of authenticity.

English spelling corrections: Forbearing and Repentance. The short meaning
for As Samad now reflects the explanation that all turn to Him in their needs.
